Computational Methodology

All calculations follow IEC 61724-1:2021 — Photovoltaic System Performance. Irradiance data (GHI) is sourced from the NASA POWER Climatology API (30-year average, ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN parameter). Input parameters are at Standard Test Conditions (STC): 1000 W/m², 25°C, AM 1.5.

System Sizing
Panel Array Area
A = (N × Wp) ÷ (1000 × η)
N = number of panels  ·  Wp = panel rated power (W)  ·  η = panel conversion efficiency (decimal)
IEC 61724-1:2021 §6.3  ·  Unit: m²
System DC Capacity
P_dc = (N × Wp) ÷ 1000
Rated peak power of the entire panel array at STC
IEC 61724-1:2021 §6.2  ·  Unit: kWp
Inverter Sizing
P_inv = P_dc × 1.10
10% safety margin ensures inverter handles peak generation without clipping
NEC Article 690 — Solar Photovoltaic Systems  ·  Unit: kVA
Monthly Energy Yield
Monthly DC Energy
E_dc = GHI × A × η × d
GHI = global horizontal irradiance (kWh/m²/day) from NASA POWER  ·  A = panel area (m²)  ·  η = panel efficiency  ·  d = days in month
IEC 61724-1:2021 §7.3  ·  Unit: kWh/month
Monthly AC Energy
E_ac = E_dc × η_inv × (1 − L)
η_inv = inverter efficiency (decimal)  ·  L = system losses fraction (wiring, soiling, mismatch, temperature)
IEC 61724-1:2021 §7.4  ·  Unit: kWh/month
Annual AC Yield
E_annual = Σ E_ac (Jan–Dec)
Sum of all twelve monthly AC energy values
IEC 61724-1:2021 §8.2  ·  Unit: kWh/year
Economic Analysis
Annual Financial Savings
S = E_annual × T
T = electricity tariff (₱/kWh)  ·  Based on avoided grid energy cost
DOE Philippines Tariff Standards  ·  Unit: ₱/year
Simple Payback Period
PBP = C ÷ S
C = total installation cost (₱)  ·  S = annual savings (₱/yr)  ·  Classification: Excellent ≤5 yr, Good 5–10 yr, Moderate 10–15 yr, Poor >15 yr
Standard Financial Engineering Practice  ·  Unit: years
Environmental Assessment
Annual CO₂ Offset
CO₂ = E_annual × 0.7
0.7 kg CO₂/kWh = Philippine grid average emission intensity factor (DOE PH, 2023)
Philippine Grid Emission Factor  ·  Unit: kg CO₂/year
